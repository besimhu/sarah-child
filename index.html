A VIEW FROM ABOVE

Glaciers are always
in motion. We’re learning why.

Using satellite imagery, historical records, and careful observation, I trace the changing speed, shape, and edges of glaciers. These patterns help us understand how ice sheets respond to the forces around them—and how that response may affect future sea levels.

02 / THE SCIENTIST

Questions
that lead
to the ice.

Sarah Child is a glaciologist and geospatial scientist whose work follows the changing dynamics of glaciers in the polar regions. She earned a PhD in Geology from the University of Kansas, an MSc in Geographic Information Science from the University of Edinburgh, and a BS in Geographic Information Systems from SUNY Cortland.

Her path to the ice began with a love of maps and the landscapes they help us see. Today, she brings satellite data and a long view to questions about glacier behavior and sea level rise.
